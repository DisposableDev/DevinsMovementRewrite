This is a conversion of the Mii Channel Theme
It uses Soundbank 14 (Inside Castle).
Based of a Midi by iteachvader(Youtube) from haminet.com
Conversion to SM64 by TheNail

You can use all of my songs in your romhacks.
You don't have to credit me (allthough I wouldn't mind being credited either).
